# TrickBook — Skateboarding: Level 1

**Working subtitle:** *The Illustrated Beginner's Guide to Tricks, Gear, Parks, Culture, and Skateboarding Life*

## Product vision

This book is the field guide a new skateboarder wishes they had: part instruction manual, part cultural handbook, part illustrated history, and part invitation into the skateboarding community.

It should help a reader understand a skateboard, ride with confidence, begin foundational tricks, enter a skatepark without feeling lost, recognize the culture around them, and know what to learn next.

It should not read like an encyclopedia or a collection of database entries. TrickBook's knowledge base supplies the facts, relationships, riders, events, tricks, parks, and references; the book turns that material into a deliberate beginner journey.

## Intended reader

- A teenager or adult beginning from zero
- A returning skater who wants to rebuild the fundamentals
- A parent trying to understand a young skater's interests
- A casual skater who knows a few tricks but not the wider culture

## Core promise

By the end of Level 1, the reader should be able to:

- Identify and choose the major parts of a skateboard
- Assemble, adjust, and inspect a basic setup
- Push, turn, stop, fall, and navigate common terrain more safely
- Practice a curated set of foundational tricks
- Read a skatepark and follow its unwritten traffic rules
- Understand foundational trick, stance, obstacle, and contest terminology
- Recognize important eras, riders, companies, places, and cultural practices
- Build a realistic plan for their next 30 sessions

## Format

- Approximately 160–220 illustrated pages; final length depends on illustration density
- Short sections designed for both sequential reading and quick reference
- Original minimalist comics, diagrams, handwritten callouts, and visual jokes
- Consistent trick pages with prerequisites, steps, mistakes, drills, and QR links
- QR links into current TrickBook trick, rider, event, park, and video content
- Digital-first production with a print-ready edition kept in mind

## Editorial principle

The book teaches someone how to join skateboarding, not merely how to perform tricks. Riding ability, safety, etiquette, history, creativity, and culture all belong in Level 1.
